Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Summary: Kagazly: PDF & Doc Scanner processes your documents on your device. Scans, visiting cards, PDFs, and edited files are created and stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. We do not operate any server that receives or stores your documents, and we never sell your files or use their contents for advertising. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which never has access to your documents.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Kagazly: PDF & Doc Scanner ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Application on iPhone and iPad.

The Application lets you scan documents and visiting cards, and create, edit, merge, split, and manage PDF files. We do not require you to create an account with us, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or any identity verification to use the Application.

2. iOS Permissions We Request

The Application requests the following system permissions only when you use a feature that needs them:

📷 Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription)

Required to scan documents, receipts, and visiting cards with your device camera. Camera images are processed on your device to detect page edges, crop, enhance, and convert them into PDFs or images. They are never uploaded to our servers, because we do not operate any.

🖼️ Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription / NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription)

Used to import existing photos you select to turn into scans or PDFs, and to save scanned pages to your Photos if you choose. The Application accesses only the photos you pick or save.

👤 Contacts (NSContactsUsageDescription)

Used only if you choose to save details from a scanned visiting card (such as name, phone number, email address, company, and address) to your device's address book. The Application does not read, upload, or share your existing contacts.

🎯 App Tracking Transparency (NSUserTrackingUsageDescription)

The Application may ask for permission to allow tracking. If you allow it, our advertising partner Google AdMob may access your device's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) to show more relevant ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. This permission has no connection to your documents. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The Application does not request access to your microphone or location.

3. How Your Documents Are Handled

Every feature below runs on your device, only when you use it:

📄 Document Scanning & Text Recognition

Scanned pages are cropped, enhanced, and saved on your device. If you use text recognition (OCR), the text is extracted on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks and is not sent to us or any third-party service.

💼 Visiting Card Scanning

Details read from a visiting card are shown to you for review on your device. They are saved to your contacts or to the Application only when you choose to do so.

🛠️ PDF Tools (Edit, Merge, Split & More)

Editing, annotating, signing, merging, splitting, reordering, compressing, converting, and password-protecting PDFs all happen on your device. Files you import through the iOS Files app or share sheet are read only to perform the action you choose.

📤 Sharing & Exporting

When you share or export a file (for example, to Files, iCloud Drive, email, AirDrop, WhatsApp, or a printer), you choose the destination through the iOS share sheet. Once shared, the file is handled by the app or service you chose, under that service's own privacy policy.

Documents often contain personal or sensitive information, such as IDs, bank statements, contracts, or medical records. We never access their contents. Please store and share them carefully.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the Application displays ads served by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK does not have access to your camera, scans, PDFs, visiting cards, or contacts. To deliver and measure ads, it may automatically collect certain technical data, including:

  • Device brand, model, operating system version, and system language.
  • IP address (used to infer approximate region for ad delivery).
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking through App Tracking Transparency.
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and load and performance diagnostics).

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies, not stored by us. See Google's Advertising Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

The Application offers optional premium subscription plans that unlock additional features and remove ads.

  • Payment Processing: All transactions, billing, receipt validation, and subscription management are handled directly by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit).
  • No Payment Data Stored: We do not collect, process, or store credit card details, billing addresses, or banking information.
  • Subscription Control: You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device.

6. Analytics & Diagnostics

Apart from the Google Mobile Ads SDK described above, the Application does not include third-party analytics SDKs. If you choose to share crash reports and diagnostics with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymized crash and performance data. This data does not include your documents and is used only to fix bugs and improve stability.

7. Data Retention, Security & Deletion

Your documents remain on your device, in your personal iCloud or device backup (if enabled), and in any locations you choose to save or share them. We operate no databases or cloud servers for your files, so we hold no copy that could be lost or breached on our side.

  • You can delete any scan or document inside the Application at any time.
  • Deleting the Application removes all documents stored inside it. Files you saved to Photos, Files, iCloud Drive, or other apps remain there until you delete them.
  • Data collected by Google AdMob is retained under Google's policies. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit tracking in your iOS privacy settings.

8. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party ad services, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in app functionality, SDK updates, or App Store requirements. Any changes will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last Updated" date.
