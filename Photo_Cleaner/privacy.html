🧹 DecluttrPix: Photo Storage Cleaner

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Summary: DecluttrPix: Photo Storage Cleaner helps you find and remove duplicate, similar, and unwanted photos and videos. To do this, it needs access to your photo library, and all scanning and analysis happen entirely on your device. Your photos and videos are never uploaded, copied to, or stored on any server we operate. The app does not require an account. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may collect limited device data as described in Section 4.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how DecluttrPix: Photo Storage Cleaner ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Application on iPhone and iPad.

The Application is a photo and video storage cleaning utility. It works entirely on your device. We do not require account creation, login credentials, email sign-ups, or identity verification to use any of its features.

2. How the Application Works on Your Device

The following features run locally on your iPhone or iPad. None of them send your photos, videos, or personal data to us:

🔍 Photo & Video Scanning

The Application scans your photo library to find duplicate and similar photos, screenshots, blurry shots, and large videos. This analysis uses Apple's on-device frameworks (such as Photos and Vision) and runs locally. Your photos, videos, and their contents are never uploaded or transmitted.

🗑️ Reviewing & Deleting Items

The Application only deletes photos or videos that you select and confirm. iOS asks you to approve each deletion, and deleted items move to the "Recently Deleted" album in the Photos app, where you can recover them for a limited time as managed by iOS.

📊 Storage Overview

The Application may read your device's available and used storage, and the file sizes of items in your library, to show how much space you can free up. This information stays on your device.

⚙️ Preferences & Scan Results

Your settings, and any cached scan results used to speed up future scans, are saved locally on your device. They may be included in your personal iCloud or device backup if you have backups enabled in iOS.

3. iOS Permissions

The Application requests the following system permissions:

🖼️ Photo Library Access (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription)

Required so the Application can scan your photos and videos for clutter and delete the items you choose. You can grant full or limited access, and you can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. Photo library data is processed only on your device.

🎯 App Tracking Transparency (NSUserTrackingUsageDescription)

In line with Apple's guidelines, the Application may ask for your permission to allow tracking. If you allow it, our advertising partner Google AdMob may access your device's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) to show more relevant, personalized ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The Application does not request access to:

  • Camera or microphone
  • Location services
  • Contacts, calendars, or reminders
  • Health or motion data

We do not sell your personal information, and your photos and videos are never shared with advertisers or any other third party.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the Application displays ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect certain technical data, including:

  • Device brand, model, operating system version, and system language.
  • IP address (used to infer approximate region for ad delivery).
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking through App Tracking Transparency.
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and load and performance diagnostics).

The Google Mobile Ads SDK does not have access to your photo library. This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies, not stored by us. To learn how Google uses advertising data, see Google's Advertising Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Upgrading to Plus removes ads from the Application.

5. In-App Subscriptions & Purchases (Plus)

The Application offers optional premium upgrades ("Plus"), which may be auto-renewing subscriptions and/or one-time in-app purchases that unlock unlimited cleaning, advanced scan features, and remove ads.

  • Payment Processing: All transactions, billing, receipt validation, and subscription management are handled directly by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit).
  • No Payment Data Stored: We do not collect, process, or store credit card details, billing addresses, or banking information. All billing data is handled exclusively by Apple Inc.
  • Purchase Status: The Application checks your purchase status with Apple on your device to unlock Plus features. We receive no personal information from Apple about you as part of this process.
  • Subscription Control: You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple ID settings on your iOS or iPadOS device (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

6. Analytics & Diagnostics

Apart from the Google Mobile Ads SDK described above, the Application does not include third-party analytics SDKs. If you choose to share crash reports and diagnostics with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymized crash and performance data. This data does not identify you, does not include your photos or videos, and is used only to fix bugs and improve stability.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

All preferences and cached scan results remain stored on your physical device or within your private iCloud backup (if enabled by you). We operate no databases, cloud storage, or backup servers for user data, and we never hold copies of your photos or videos. Deleting the Application from your device removes all of its locally stored data; it does not affect the photos and videos in your library. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained under Google's policies; you can reset your advertising identifier or limit tracking in your iOS privacy settings.

8. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party ad services, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in app functionality or App Store requirements. Any changes will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last Updated" date.
