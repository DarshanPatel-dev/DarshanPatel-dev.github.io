Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Summary: Clarivo: Hearing Amplifier processes microphone audio in real time on your device and plays it back through your headphones, AirPods, or Bluetooth device. We do not record, store, upload, or listen to your audio, and we never sell your data. Hearing test results and sound settings stay on your iPhone or iPad. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which never has access to your microphone or audio.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Clarivo: Hearing Amplifier ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Application on iPhone and iPad.

The Application captures sound from your device's microphone, amplifies and enhances it, and plays it to your connected headphones, AirPods, or Bluetooth audio device. It also offers a hearing test and sound personalization. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or any identity verification to use the Application.

2. iOS Permissions We Request

The Application requests the following system permissions only when you use a feature that needs them:

🎙️ Microphone (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription)

Required to capture the sound around you so it can be amplified and played through your headphones. Audio is processed in real time, in memory, on your device. It is not recorded, saved, transmitted, or analyzed for its content, and it is never sent to us or any third party.

🎧 Bluetooth & Audio Output

The Application plays amplified sound through the audio output you choose, such as AirPods, wired headphones, or other Bluetooth headphones and earbuds, using Apple's standard audio routing. If the Application asks for Bluetooth access (NSBluetoothAlwaysUsageDescription), it is used only to detect and connect to your audio device. We do not collect the names or identifiers of your Bluetooth devices.

🎯 App Tracking Transparency (NSUserTrackingUsageDescription)

The Application may ask for permission to allow tracking. If you allow it, our advertising partner Google AdMob may access your device's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) to show more relevant ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. This permission has no connection to your microphone or audio. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The Application does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, or photo library.

3. How Your Audio & Hearing Data Are Handled

Every feature below runs on your device, only when you use it:

🔊 Live Amplification & Sound Boost

Sound from the microphone is amplified, equalized, and filtered (for example, noise reduction and voice enhancement) in real time and sent straight to your headphones. The audio is held in memory only for the fraction of a second needed to process it and is then discarded.

🌙 Background Listening

If you keep amplification running while the Application is in the background or your screen is locked, iOS shows the microphone indicator so you always know the microphone is active. You can stop it at any time from the Application or Control Center.

📈 Hearing Test

The hearing test plays tones at different frequencies and volumes and records only your responses (whether you heard each tone). The results and the sound profile created from them are stored on your device. They are not sent to us, shared with advertisers, or written to Apple Health.

🎚️ Settings & Presets

Your volume, equalizer, ear balance, and preset choices are saved on your device so the Application remembers them next time.

Hearing test results are personal health-related information. Because they never leave your device through the Application, only you and anyone with access to your device can see them.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the Application displays ads served by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK does not have access to your microphone, live audio, hearing test results, or sound settings. To deliver and measure ads, it may automatically collect certain technical data, including:

  • Device brand, model, operating system version, and system language.
  • IP address (used to infer approximate region for ad delivery).
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking through App Tracking Transparency.
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and load and performance diagnostics).

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies, not stored by us. See Google's Advertising Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

The Application offers optional premium subscription plans that unlock additional features and remove ads.

  • Payment Processing: All transactions, billing, receipt validation, and subscription management are handled directly by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit).
  • No Payment Data Stored: We do not collect, process, or store credit card details, billing addresses, or banking information.
  • Subscription Control: You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device.

6. Analytics & Diagnostics

Apart from the Google Mobile Ads SDK described above, the Application does not include third-party analytics SDKs. If you choose to share crash reports and diagnostics with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymized crash and performance data. This data does not include any audio or hearing test results and is used only to fix bugs and improve stability.

7. Data Retention, Security & Deletion

Live audio is never stored. Hearing test results and settings remain on your device and in your personal iCloud or device backup (if enabled). We operate no databases or cloud servers for your data, so we hold no copy that could be lost or breached on our side.

  • You can delete or retake your hearing test at any time inside the Application.
  • Deleting the Application removes all hearing test results and settings stored inside it.
  • Data collected by Google AdMob is retained under Google's policies. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit tracking in your iOS privacy settings.

8. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party ad services, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in app functionality, SDK updates, or App Store requirements. Any changes will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last Updated" date.
