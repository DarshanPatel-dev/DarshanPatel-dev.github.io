Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Summary: Spendlio Expense Manager keeps your financial records on your device. The expenses, income, categories, budgets, and notes you enter are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad. We do not operate any server that receives or stores your financial data, we do not connect to your bank accounts, and we never sell your data or use it for advertising. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which never has access to your expense records.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendlio Expense Manager ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Application on iPhone and iPad.

The Application helps you record and track your expenses, organize them into categories, and review your spending over time. We do not require you to create an account with us, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or any identity verification to use the Application.

2. Information You Enter

To track your spending, you may enter information such as:

  • Expense and income amounts, dates, and currencies.
  • Categories, payment methods, and account or wallet names you create.
  • Notes, descriptions, and tags you add to transactions.
  • Budgets, spending limits, and recurring expense settings.

This information is stored only on your device and is used only to show your transactions, totals, budgets, charts, and reports inside the Application. We never see it. Please avoid entering sensitive details such as full card numbers, bank account numbers, or passwords in notes or descriptions.

3. How the Application Works on Your Device

📒 Expense Tracking & Reports

Totals, category breakdowns, charts, and spending reports are calculated on your device from the data you enter. No financial data is sent to us or to any analytics service to produce them.

🏦 No Bank Connections

The Application does not connect to your bank, card, or payment accounts, and does not ask for banking login credentials. All transactions are entered by you.

💾 Backups & Exports

Your data may be included in your personal iCloud or device backup if you have backups enabled in iOS. If the Application lets you export your records (for example, as a CSV or PDF file), the file is created on your device and you choose where to save or share it. Once shared, the file is handled by the app or service you chose, under that service's own privacy policy.

4. iOS Permissions

🎯 App Tracking Transparency (NSUserTrackingUsageDescription)

The Application may ask for permission to allow tracking. If you allow it, our advertising partner Google AdMob may access your device's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) to show more relevant ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. This permission has no connection to your expense data. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The Application does not request access to your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, or location.

5. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the Application displays ads served by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK does not have access to your expenses, income, budgets, or any other financial records you enter. To deliver and measure ads, it may automatically collect certain technical data, including:

  • Device brand, model, operating system version, and system language.
  • IP address (used to infer approximate region for ad delivery).
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking through App Tracking Transparency.
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and load and performance diagnostics).

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies, not stored by us. See Google's Advertising Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases

The Application offers optional premium subscription plans that unlock additional features and remove ads.

  • Payment Processing: All transactions, billing, receipt validation, and subscription management are handled directly by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit).
  • No Payment Data Stored: We do not collect, process, or store credit card details, billing addresses, or banking information.
  • Subscription Control: You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device.

7. Analytics & Diagnostics

Apart from the Google Mobile Ads SDK described above, the Application does not include third-party analytics SDKs. If you choose to share crash reports and diagnostics with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymized crash and performance data. This data does not include your financial records and is used only to fix bugs and improve stability.

8. Data Retention, Security & Deletion

Your financial records remain on your device, in your personal iCloud or device backup (if enabled), and in any files you choose to export. We operate no databases or cloud servers for your data, so we hold no copy of your records that could be lost or breached on our side.

  • You can edit or delete individual transactions, categories, and budgets at any time in the Application.
  • Deleting the Application from your device removes all of its locally stored data. This cannot be undone unless you have a backup or exported file.
  • Data collected by Google AdMob is retained under Google's policies. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit tracking in your iOS privacy settings.

9. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party ad services, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in app functionality, SDK updates, or App Store requirements. Any changes will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last Updated" date.
