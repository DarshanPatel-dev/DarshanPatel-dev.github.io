📷 GPS Map Camera: Stamp & Geo

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Summary: GPS Map Camera: Stamp & Geo is designed as an offline-first, on-device utility. We do not operate remote servers to harvest or store your photos, videos, or exact location coordinates. All stamp rendering is processed locally on your hardware.

1. Overview & Architecture

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which GPS Map Camera: Stamp & Geo ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") handles data.

The Application functions primarily as a local device utility. We do not require account creation, login credentials, email sign-ups, or personal identity verification to use the application's core stamping features.

2. iOS Device Permissions We Request

To stamp geographical context, dates, and addresses onto your images and videos, the Application requests explicit system permissions. Here is why each permission is required and how it is used:

📷 Camera Permission (NSCameraUsageDescription)

Required to access your device camera viewfinder, capture high-resolution photos, and record video frames with your chosen real-time stamp templates. Media is captured directly inside the app session.

🖼️ Photo Library Permission (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription / Add-Only)

Required to save the stamped photos and recorded videos directly to your device’s native Photos album. The Application does not transmit your personal photo library to any remote server or third party.

🎙️ Microphone Permission (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription)

Required solely to capture synchronous audio when you choose to record video with the camera. Audio data is rendered locally into the output video file and is never uploaded or analyzed.

📍 Location Services (NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription)

Used exclusively while the app is active to retrieve your current GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude) and perform reverse geocoding to display your city, address, or map snippet on the camera stamp overlay. Your location coordinates are processed on-device and are not collected into a tracking database.

🎯 App Tracking Transparency (NSUserTrackingUsageDescription)

In accordance with Apple guidelines, if you grant permission, third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdMob) may access your device's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) to serve personalized, relevant advertisements. If you decline or deny this request, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized contextual ads.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free tier of the Application displays third-party advertisements served by Google AdMob. To deliver ad units, SDK libraries may automatically collect certain pseudonymous technical data, including:

  • Device brand, model, operating system version, and system language.
  • IP address (used to infer coarse regional location for ad delivery).
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) — strictly subject to your App Tracking permission choice.
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, and load diagnostics).

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, review the Google Ad & Privacy Policy.

4. In-App Subscriptions & Purchases

The Application offers optional premium auto-renewing subscriptions and/or in-app purchases (e.g., to unlock premium stamp designs, export high-bitrate video, or remove all advertisements).

  • Payment Processing: All financial transactions, billing, receipt validation, and subscription management are conducted directly through Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit).
  • No Payment Data Stored: We do not collect, process, or store credit card details, billing addresses, or banking information. All billing data is secured exclusively by Apple Inc.
  • Subscription Control: You can manage, cancel, or modify your subscription at any time via your Apple ID Account Settings on your iOS device.

5. Data Retention & On-Device Security

All photographs, video recordings, audio tracks, and customized stamp configurations remain stored exclusively on your physical iOS device or within your private Apple iCloud backup (if enabled by you). We operate no independent databases, cloud storage buckets, or secondary backup servers for user media.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe an underage child has provided personal details through third-party ad services, please contact us so appropriate measures can be taken.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in app functionality, SDK updates, or Apple Store Review requirements. Any changes will be posted directly to this URL with a revised "Last Updated" timestamp.
