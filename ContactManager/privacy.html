C Contaq: Contact Backup & Merge

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Summary: Contaq: Contact Backup & Merge works on your device. Your contacts are read, exported, imported, merged, and deleted locally on your iPhone or iPad. We do not operate any server that receives or stores your contacts, and we never sell, share, or use your contact data for advertising. When you choose to sync with Google Contacts, your data moves directly between your device and Google. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which never has access to your contacts.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Contaq: Contact Backup & Merge ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Application on iPhone and iPad.

The Application helps you back up, export, import, sync, merge, and clean up your contacts. We do not require you to create an account with us, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or any identity verification to use the Application.

2. iOS Permissions We Request

The Application requests the following system permissions. Here is why each one is needed and how it is used:

👥 Contacts Access (NSContactsUsageDescription)

Required to read, back up, export, import, merge, and delete contacts in your device's address book. Contact data (such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses, companies, notes, birthdays, and contact photos) is processed only on your device to perform the actions you choose. It is never uploaded to our servers, because we do not operate any.

🎯 App Tracking Transparency (NSUserTrackingUsageDescription)

The Application may ask for permission to allow tracking. If you allow it, our advertising partner Google AdMob may access your device's Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) to show more relevant ads. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. This permission has no connection to your contacts. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The Application does not request access to your camera, microphone, photo library, or location.

3. How Your Contacts Are Used

Every action below happens only when you start it, and runs on your device:

📤 Export (PDF, Excel / CSV, VCF)

The Application creates PDF, CSV, or vCard (VCF) files from the contacts you select. These files are generated on your device. You decide where they go using the iOS share sheet or Files app (for example, saving to Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email, or another app). Once you share an exported file, it is handled by the app or service you chose, under that service's own privacy policy. Exported files contain personal information, so please store and share them carefully.

📥 Import (Excel / CSV, VCF)

You can import contacts from CSV or VCF files that you pick through the iOS file picker. The Application reads only the files you select, and only to add their contacts to your address book. Imported files are not uploaded anywhere.

🔄 Google Contacts Sync

If you choose to connect a Google account, you sign in through Google's own secure sign-in page. With your permission, the Application uses the Google People API to read and write your Google Contacts so it can sync them with your device. Contact data travels directly between your device and Google over an encrypted connection and never passes through our servers. Your Google sign-in token is stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device. You can disconnect at any time in the Application, or remove access from your Google Account permissions page.

🧩 Duplicate Detection & Merge

The Application compares names, phone numbers, and email addresses on your device to find duplicate contacts and merge them when you confirm. This analysis is performed entirely on your device.

🗑️ Multiple Delete

You can select and delete several contacts at once. Deletion happens only after you confirm it, and is carried out on your device's address book (and on Google Contacts, if that account is synced). We recommend exporting a backup before deleting contacts.

4. Google API Services — Limited Use Disclosure

The Application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Google Contacts data is used only to provide the sync, backup, export, merge, and delete features you request in the Application.
  • Google Contacts data is never transferred to us or to any third party, except as needed to provide those features, to comply with applicable law, or with your explicit consent.
  • Google Contacts data is never used or sold for advertising, including personalized or retargeted ads, and is never shared with Google AdMob or any advertising partner.
  • No human reads your Google Contacts data, except with your explicit consent (for example, if you send it to us for support), for security purposes, or to comply with law.

5. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the Application displays ads served by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK does not have access to your contacts. To deliver and measure ads, it may automatically collect certain technical data, including:

  • Device brand, model, operating system version, and system language.
  • IP address (used to infer approximate region for ad delivery).
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking through App Tracking Transparency.
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and load and performance diagnostics).

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies, not stored by us. See Google's Advertising Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. In-App Subscriptions & Purchases

The Application may offer optional premium upgrades, as auto-renewing subscriptions and/or one-time in-app purchases, that unlock additional features and remove ads.

  • Payment Processing: All transactions, billing, receipt validation, and subscription management are handled directly by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit).
  • No Payment Data Stored: We do not collect, process, or store credit card details, billing addresses, or banking information.
  • Subscription Control: You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device.

7. Data Retention, Security & Deletion

Your contacts stay in your device's address book, in files you export, and in Google Contacts if you sync. We operate no databases, cloud storage, or backup servers for your data, so we hold no copy of your contacts that could be lost or breached on our side.

  • Deleting the Application removes its local settings and any backup files stored inside the Application. Contacts in your address book and in Google Contacts are not affected.
  • Exported files you saved to Files, iCloud Drive, or other apps remain there until you delete them.
  • To revoke Google access, disconnect your account in the Application or visit your Google Account permissions page.

8. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party ad services, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in app functionality, SDK updates, or App Store and Google requirements. Any changes will be posted at this URL with a revised "Last Updated" date.
